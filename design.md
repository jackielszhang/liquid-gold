# Liquid Gold visual direction

A serious fuel instrument panel. Dark by default, warm off white on near black, with monospaced type throughout.

## Palette

- Background: `#131413`
- Panel: `#202220`
- Ink: `#EFEADC`
- Secondary text: `#A5A59D`
- Rules and plotting grid: `#4F524D`

## Rules

- Large, bold readouts carry the hierarchy. Small uppercase labels identify each instrument.
- Square panels and fine rules organize data. No rounded cards, shadows, or decorative gradients.
- Selected controls invert to off white. The palette stays monochrome.
- The price vector uses the projected cents-per-litre change. Its line travels vertically up to 100c of change; the exact value remains visible beside it.
- Dashboard, setup, settings, and widgets share the same palette and typography.
